## Long description

NOVA is a personal AI assistant that runs entirely on your own machine. You chat with open models (Google Gemma 4, Alibaba Qwen, OpenAI gpt-oss) through the llama.cpp engine on your GPU. NOVA installs them in one click, but only when you decide to.

Your conversations, settings and memory are stored locally. Nothing leaves your computer unless you ask for something that needs the internet (weather, a Wikipedia check, a model download), and those answers are clearly labelled.

NOVA also ships **Nova 1**, a family of small models and tools written and trained from scratch for this project, with no external weights. Nova 1:
- answers everyday requests in under a millisecond;
- recognises animals, objects and landscapes in photos and videos;
- draws simple illustrations from a sentence;
- summarises PDFs.

Around the chat there are built-in tools that answer instantly and offline:
- maths with steps, conversions and statistics;
- weather;
- file search in folders you allow;
- computer actions, which run only after you click **Allow**.

**Nova Code** adds a coding workspace with diff review before any change is written. On macOS, a notch island and a menu-bar mini chat keep NOVA one hover away.

NOVA is free, needs no account, and is open source under the MIT license.
